# Evaluation Rubric

Each response is scored on a 0-10 scale.

## Dimension 1

Accuracy

Does the answer correctly interpret the scenario?

## Dimension 2

Evidence Awareness

Does the answer distinguish evidence from assumptions?

## Dimension 3

Compliance Reasoning

Does the answer follow governance and process requirements?

## Dimension 4

Risk Awareness

Does the model recognise uncertainty and risk exposure?

## Dimension 5

Actionability

Does the response provide meaningful next steps?

---

## Critical Error Rule

Any response committing a critical judgement failure receives:

Score = 0

Examples:

- Treating incomplete verification as complete
- Claiming compliance without evidence
- Ignoring safety-critical risk
- Accepting supplier claims without validation
